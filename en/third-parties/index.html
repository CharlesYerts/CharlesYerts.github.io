4. Services not currently integrated

selfo 1.2 currently does not integrate:

  • advertising SDKs or ad networks;
  • third-party analytics such as Firebase Analytics or Google Analytics;
  • third-party crash reporting such as Firebase Crashlytics or Sentry;
  • attribution SDKs such as Adjust, AppsFlyer, or Branch;
  • social login or Sign in with Apple;
  • a developer-operated account, media cloud sync, or media backup backend; or
  • cross-app or cross-website behavioral tracking.

5. Changes to this list

If a future version adds an SDK, cloud service, account, analytics, crash reporting, remote configuration, or another recipient, we will update this list and the Privacy Policy before enabling it and provide notice or obtain consent where required.
