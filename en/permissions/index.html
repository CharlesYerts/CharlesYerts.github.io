A feature-by-feature list of system permission requested by selfo, information processed on device, and the effect of refusing or withdrawing access.

1. System permission

On first launch, selfo shows the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use. selfo requests read-and-write Photos access only after you tap “Agree and Continue”; tapping “Not Now” does not request Photos access.

3. Permissions not currently requested

selfo currently does not request:

  • precise or background device location;
  • camera or microphone;
  • contacts, calendar, or reminders;
  • notifications;
  • Bluetooth or local network;
  • health or motion data; or
  • advertising identifier or App Tracking Transparency permission.

Reading capture coordinates already embedded in a media file is not the same as requesting device location permission.

4. Your controls

  • Change or withdraw selfo's Photos access in iOS Settings.
  • Undo an unconfirmed favorite or deletion mark on a pending page.
  • Remove album membership from “selfo 收藏” without deleting the original.
  • Manage system-deleted media in Recently Deleted in Apple Photos.
  • Delete selfo to remove most local app records.
  • Manage subscriptions through your Apple account.
  • Email a privacy request about support communications.

5. Required Reason APIs

selfo's Apple privacy manifest declares no tracking and no collected data types. It uses UserDefaults for local preferences and reads system boot time for timing or performance-related logic. These accesses are declared under Apple's Required Reason API rules and are not used to identify, profile, or track users across apps.
