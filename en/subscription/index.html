Subscription Disclosure

selfo Pro is an auto-renewable subscription offered through Apple's App Store. A purchase does not change selfo's on-device media processing.

1. Free version

The free version can organize 20 photos and 10 videos each day. The allowance resets automatically at the start of each local calendar day. Adding an item to Pending Favorites or Pending Deletion reserves that day's corresponding allowance; withdrawing it before confirmation releases the allowance, and a successful favorite or deletion then counts as used for that day. The same media item is counted once across organization entry points or when switching between favorite and delete, and a left-swipe skip does not count. An item added to a pending queue on a previous day can still be confirmed after the day changes and does not use the new day's allowance.

2. selfo Pro benefits

While active, selfo Pro removes the daily free photo and video organization limits across every organization entry point. Every subscription period unlocks the same benefits.

A subscription changes feature entitlement only. Core photo and video analysis remains on device, and subscribing does not upload media to a developer-controlled selfo server.

4. Payment, auto-renewal, and cancellation

  • Apple charges your Apple account after you confirm purchase.
  • The subscription automatically renews unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • Apple ordinarily confirms renewal and charges the then-displayed price within 24 hours before the current period ends.
  • Cancel, renew, or change a plan in iOS Settings or Apple subscription management.
  • Deleting selfo does not cancel a subscription.

5. Restore, billing, and refunds

If you change devices, reinstall, or do not see an entitlement, select Restore Purchases on selfo's subscription page. Use the Apple account that made the purchase and ensure network access.

Apple manages payment methods, billing, charges, cancellation, and refunds. Request a refund through Apple's Report a Problem or Apple Support. The developer cannot view complete payment credentials, change a payment method, or approve a refund.
