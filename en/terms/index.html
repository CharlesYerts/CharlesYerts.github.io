4. Organization actions and confirmation

4.1 Gestures and pending queues

  • A left swipe skips the current item without adding it to favorite or deletion queues and without consuming the free organization quota.
  • An upward swipe adds an item to pending favorites; a right swipe adds it to pending deletion. Both are local marks that require another confirmation on a review page.
  • Scan and grouping results are suggestions and never favorite or delete media automatically.

4.2 “selfo 收藏”

Confirming a favorite adds the original photo or video to an ordinary system album named “selfo 收藏.” It creates no copy, does not move the original, and does not set the Apple Photos heart state. Removing an item from that album only removes album membership and does not delete the library original.

4.3 Deletion and recovery

After you confirm deletion, selfo asks PhotoKit to delete the item and iOS displays a system confirmation. Successfully deleted media ordinarily enters Recently Deleted in Apple Photos; Apple controls its retention and recovery. If you cancel or the system operation fails, selfo does not force deletion.

Review carefully and back up important content

Algorithms, file-size estimates, similarity judgments, and groups may be incomplete or inaccurate. selfo does not guarantee that every relevant item will be identified or that deleted content will always be recoverable.
