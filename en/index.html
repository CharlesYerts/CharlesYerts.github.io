  1. 01

    Choose where to begin

    Start with a photo or video album, or open a focused tool for similar shots, captures, color themes, or large files.

  2. 02

    Make quick decisions

    Swipe to move on, queue a favorite, or queue a deletion. Scanning tools only find and group; they never modify your library automatically.

  3. 03

    Review and confirm

    Preview, select, or undo from the pending pages. selfo only favorites or requests deletion after your final confirmation.

Core organization, on your device

Privacy is not an option.
It is the default.

Core scanning and organization happen on device. Photo and video content is not uploaded to a developer-controlled media server, and no selfo account is required.

ON

On-device analysis

Core scanning and organization run directly on your iPhone.

NO

No selfo account

There is no developer account to create, register, or sign in to.

0

No ad or third-party analytics SDKs

The current app includes no advertising, third-party analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs.

YOU

No cross-app tracking

selfo does not request tracking permission or build cross-app advertising profiles.

Media stored only in iCloud may be downloaded by Apple Photos. Subscriptions and place-name lookup may also connect to Apple services. Read the full Privacy Policy.
