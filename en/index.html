04 / REVIEW FIRST

BROWSE · REVIEW · COMPLETE

Three steps from clutter to clarity.

selfo breaks organization into calm, reversible steps instead of pressuring you with one-tap cleanup.

  1. 01

    Choose a place to start

    Begin with photos, videos, or any focused tool and work through one clear group.

  2. 02

    Review pending items

    Favorites and deletions stay in on-device queues until you preview, select, or undo them.

  3. 03

    Confirm the final action

    Organization becomes final only after you confirm a favorite or ask iOS to delete.

PRIVACY BY DEFAULT

Core organization stays on your device.

selfo performs core scanning and organization locally on iPhone. Photo and video content is not uploaded to a developer-controlled media server, and no selfo account is required.

ON

Core processing on device

Core scanning, comparison, and organization records stay on this iPhone.

NO

No proprietary account

Open the app and start without registering or signing into a developer account.

0

The current version has no advertising, third-party analytics, or crash-reporting SDK.
